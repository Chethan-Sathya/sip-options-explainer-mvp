# Usability study: AI explanation versus non-AI help pages

**Objective:** Determine whether a source-grounded AI explanation helps SIP investors understand existing options more accurately and quickly than opening official Groww FAQ pages. This is a prototype comparison, not an investment-outcome experiment.

## Test tasks (fictitious, no actual account required)

1. **Variable expenses:** Your travel costs increased unexpectedly. Find what SIP-management options the platform documents and any timing restrictions.
2. **Payment failure:** An automatic deduction failed. Find the documented verification or recovery path.
3. **Account uncertainty:** Your bank shows a debit but Groww does not show units. Determine what can be verified and what cannot.

## Two versions

- **AI condition:** Show the deployed SIP Options Explainer with AI enabled and a visible quality gate.
- **Non-AI baseline:** Show official Groww FAQ URLs linked in `knowledge_base.json`, without AI explanations, and ask the same question.

Alternate the order across participants when practical. Avoid conducting actual trades, payments, or edits.

## Record for each participant

- Anonymous participant code and relevant platform/experience category; consent to note-taking.
- Task ID and condition (AI or FAQ baseline).
- Task completion (yes / partly / no).
- Time to find an appropriate answer, measured consistently.
- Comprehension check: Can they state the relevant option and one important limitation, without prompting?
- Safety check: Do they mistakenly believe the assistant has checked their actual Groww account or recommended an investment?
- Evidence of confusion, quote paraphrases, and any system failures.

## Outcome and caveat

Report exact denominators, not percentages alone. Treat this as an exploratory usability study; a small convenience sample does not demonstrate improvement in 12-month SIP continuation. Do not claim any tests took place until they are completed.
